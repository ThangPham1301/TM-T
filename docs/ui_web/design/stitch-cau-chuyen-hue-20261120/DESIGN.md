## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the poetic heritage, regal subtlety, and artisanal intimacy of Huế craft traditions. It blends the solemnity of Imperial architecture with the warmth of handcrafted packaging and culinary folklore.

The design movement is **Refined Editorial Warmth** paired with **Tactile Heritage Minimalism**:
- Generous, breathable layouts evoking traditional rice paper prints and archival scrolls.
- Delicate, disciplined lines inspired by wooden lattice partitions (*hoa văn chữ thọ*, imperial screen doors).
- Tactile artisanal motifs—notably the origami/wrapped corner (*góc gấp gói quà*)—signifying thoughtful curation, royal presentation, and gift-giving reverence.
- The interface avoids overt noise or jarring digital cues, maintaining a serene, tactile, and literary rhythm throughout user flows.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the royal courts of the Perfume River, lacquered wood, and sun-dried imperial spices:

- **Primary (`#573722`)**: Deep Cacao Brown. Applied to high-emphasis buttons, prominent headers, and core brand anchors. On deep focus or pressed states, shifts toward Obsidian Cacao (`#3e220e`).
- **Secondary (`#C99643`)**: Imperial Gold (*Vàng Hoàng Triều*). Applied to refined accents, active borders, fold highlights, badges, and warm hover glimmers. Reaches `#d99938` on active interactive emphasis.
- **Tertiary / Structural Stroke (`#eadbc8`)**: Parchment Sand. A soft, warm border tone designed for subtle card outlines, fine partition lines, and tactile separation.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#fff8f5`)**: Warm Rice Cream. Serves as the global backdrop. Paired with `#fcf6f0` (Silk Paper) for layered card surfaces, trays, and inner canvas sections.
- **Typography Tone**: Primary reading text resolves strictly to `#3e220e` (Dark Cacao) for contrast and readability, while secondary supporting metadata uses `#573722` at 80% opacity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Noto Serif** for storytelling, imperial poise, and emotional resonance with **Be Vietnam Pro** for modern Vietnamese diacritical clarity and legibility.

- **Editorial Headings (`Noto Serif`)**: Set with balanced optical weight and gentle tracking. Used across banner titles, article headings, section milestones, and gift set monikers.
- **Interface & Narrative Body (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: Crafted specifically for Vietnamese orthography, preventing tone marks from colliding or feeling condensed. Used for long-form regional lore, product descriptions, prices, navigation items, and interface labels.
- **Diacritical Balance**: Ensure minimum line-heights are observed (at least 1.5x on body sizes) to honor Vietnamese diacritics without truncation or ascender crowding.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a structured **12-column desktop grid** with an intentional ceiling:
- **Max Container Width**: 1280px, horizontally centered with `margin: auto` and minimum 3.5rem outer canvas margins.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing enforces generous breathing room between curation blocks, preventing the density typical of generic commercial marketplaces. Product cards align across 3-column (4 cards per row) or 4-column (3 cards per row) compositions.
- **Editorial Split Panels**: Narrative modules (`Câu chuyện Huế`) frequently employ asymmetrical 5/7 or 4/8 column splits—pairing archival imagery or calligraphy with storytelling copy.
- **Grid Gutter**: Fixed at `1.5rem` (24px) to retain continuous structural proportion between gift boxes and image panels.

## Elevation & Depth

To echo traditional Vietnamese packaging—layered rice paper, silk linings, and lacquer trays—elevation relies on **layered tone shifts** and **soft ambient warmth** rather than stark digital drops:

- **Surface Layering**:
  - Base canvas: `#fff8f5` (Warm Rice Cream).
  - Raised containers, modal bodies, and panels: `#fcf6f0` (Lacquered Silk Paper).
  - Recessed inputs and media frames: `#f7eee5`.
- **Ambient Shadow (Gói Subtle)**:
  - Cards and floating trays feature an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(62, 34, 14, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(62, 34, 14, 0.04)`.
  - Floating sticky header and active drawers: `0 4px 20px 0 rgba(87, 55, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Boundary Precision**: All elevated elements retain a 1px perimeter border in Parchment Sand (`#eadbc8`) to maintain crisp, tactile definition against the cream ground.

## Shapes

The design system embraces disciplined, soft geometric elegance:

- Standard panels, cards, and input fields use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- Feature showcases and hero wrappers utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px).
- Interactive pills, tags, and small badges apply full pill rounding.
- **The Origami Fold Signature (`Góc Gấp Gói Quà`)**:
  - The upper-right corner of heritage cards and story panels integrates an origami folded corner motif.
  - Constructed via a subtle 45-degree diagonal fold effect (`linear-gradient(225deg, #C99643 0px, #C99643 18px, transparent 18.5px)`), creating an authentic triangular fold edge in Imperial Gold, simulating folded royal wrapping paper or an ancient book corner bookmark.

### Header (Fixed Sticky)
- **Container**: Sticky desktop bar (`height: 76px`), surface tone `#fff8f5` with 95% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`. Border bottom: 1px solid `#eadbc8`.
- **Emblem / Brand**: Left-aligned "Gói Huế" logotype in Noto Serif (weight 600, `#3e220e`) alongside a subtle gold seal mark (*Ấn triện*).
- **Navigation Links**: Centered horizontally. Labels: `'Trang chủ'`, `'Bộ sưu tập'`, `'Câu chuyện Huế'`. Font: Be Vietnam Pro (`label-lg`), `#573722`. Hover transition displays a fine 1.5px imperial gold underline indicator.
- **Search Input**: Pill-style compact search field, background `#fcf6f0`, border 1px solid `#eadbc8`, placeholder in `#573722` at 60% opacity.
- **Utility Actions**: Right-aligned user profile and shopping cart icon triggers. Cacao brown icons with an imperial gold item badge counter on the cart.

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#573722`, text `#ffffff` (`label-lg`), border: 1px solid `#573722`, border-radius `rounded-lg`. On hover: border color transitions to Imperial Gold `#C99643` with a soft gold outer glow (`0 0 0 1px #C99643, 0 4px 12px rgba(201, 150, 67, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Heritage Outline**: Background transparent, border: 1px solid `#573722`, text `#573722`. Hover: background fills with `#eadbc8` at 30% opacity, border shifts to `#C99643`.
- **Text / Story Action**: Noto Serif or Be Vietnam Pro with an inline chevron (`→`), text `#573722`, hover `#C99643`.

### Heritage Cards & Specialty Gift Panels
- **Structure**: Surface `#fcf6f0`, border 1px solid `#eadbc8`, border-radius `rounded-lg`.
- **The Fold Motif**: Top-right corner styled with the signature Imperial Gold origami angle (`#C99643`).

- **Imagery**: Soft sepia-tinted framing with warm inner borders.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Input Fields**: Background `#fcf6f0`, 1px solid `#eadbc8`, text `#3e220e`, border-radius `rounded-md`. Focus state: border shifts to `#C99643` with `outline: none` and 2px `#eadbc8` ring.

- **Chips / Tags**: Pill shapes, background `#fff8f5`, border 1px solid `#eadbc8`, label `#573722`. Active state: background `#573722`, text `#ffffff`, border `#573722`.
